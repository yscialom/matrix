Detailed Description

template<class T, std::size_t... Dimensions>
struct ysc::matrix_view< T, strided, Dimensions... >::iterator

Random-access iterator over a 1-D strided view.

Advances by _stride elements per step. Satisfies std::random_access_iterator (but not std::contiguous_iterator).
